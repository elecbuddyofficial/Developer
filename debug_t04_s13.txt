MSB Safeties — Complete List
🔴 EXAM CRITICAL: Asked by ALL surveyors — most common switchboard question
1. Dead-front panel construction: Cannot open live panel door — physical/electrical interlock prevents access to live conductors.
2. IDMT Over-current relay (OCR): Inverse Definite Minimum Time — trips FASTER for higher fault current. Protects busbars and feeders.
3. Under-voltage (UV) trip relay: Trips generator ACB if voltage drops below ~85% rated. Prevents motoring.
4. Reverse power relay: Detects power flow FROM busbar INTO generator — trips ACB. Prevents motoring of alternator.
5. Earth fault indicator lamps: Three lamps in star configuration. Normal = all glow equally. Fault = earthed phase lamp goes dim/dark.
6. Bus-tie (BT) interlock: Prevents wrong buses being paralleled. Ensures only correctly synchronised sections connected.
7. Preferential trip relay: Sheds non-essential loads sequentially (in stages) to prevent overload and blackout.
8. Standby start interlock: Auto-starts standby generator on overload or main generator failure.
9. Emergency stop push button: Trips all generator ACBs simultaneously for emergency shutdown.
10. Overvoltage relay: Trips generator if output voltage exceeds ~110% rated — protects connected equipment.
