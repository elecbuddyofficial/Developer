1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Elec-Buddy collects when you use our exam preparation platform for marine Electro-Technical Officers, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryWhat we collect
Account informationFull name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never see or store your password in plain text).
Payment informationPlan purchased, amount paid, and the order and payment identifiers returned by Razorpay. We do not collect or store your card number, UPI ID, or net banking credentials; these are handled directly by Razorpay.
Study activityWhich topics you have read, your progress through them, and your quiz results, so you can pick up where you left off and track your revision.
Device informationA device identifier used to enforce the per-account device limit described in our Terms, so that one subscription cannot be shared across an unlimited number of devices.

Usage analyticsAggregate, privacy-friendly page-view analytics that do not identify you individually or track you across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • to create and maintain your account, and to verify your identity when you sign in;
  • to grant and enforce access to the content your subscription covers;
  • to process payments and send you a receipt and confirmation of your access dates;
  • to send trial and subscription expiry reminders, and other service-related emails such as a welcome message when you sign up;
  • to save your reading progress and quiz scores across sessions and devices;
  • to enforce the device limit and detect account sharing that would breach our Terms;
  • to respond to support requests you send us;
  • to understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your study data or account details for third-party advertising.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase using industry-standard hashing.
  • Database access is restricted with row-level security, so your data is only readable by you and by authorised administrative functions, never by other subscribers.
  • Paid study content is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, so that access requires an active, verified subscription.
  • All data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Elec-Buddy uses your browser's local storage, not third-party advertising cookies, to keep the app working smoothly. This includes your signed-in session, theme preference, Data Saver setting, and your last reading position, so the app remembers your preferences and progress between visits. This data stays on your own device and is not shared with advertisers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and study data for as long as your account remains active, so that your progress and access history are preserved. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete your personal information, other than records we are required to keep for legitimate accounting, tax, or legal purposes, such as payment records.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • request a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate account information, such as your name or email address;
  • request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
Account deletion is currently handled by our support team rather than as a self-service option in the app. Email support@elec-buddy.com from your registered address and we will action your request.

9. Children's Privacy

Elec-Buddy is intended for maritime professionals and cadets preparing for ETO certification, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

10. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and study data are stored on servers located in India (Mumbai region), in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at support@elec-buddy.com.
