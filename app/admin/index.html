Upgrade Funnel

Where people stop on the way to paying, and who stopped there.

Upgrade funnel
How far people get on the way to paying, and where they stop. Counts people, not clicks, so someone who opens the window four times is one person still deciding. Each step includes everyone who got further, so the numbers only ever go down. A big drop between two steps is where the problem is: losing them at Picked a plan points at price or at the tiers being confusing, losing them at Reached the payment window points at checkout itself.

The same funnel, by person. Times counts how often they came back to look, which is the clearest signal of interest. Whichever column you sort by is the one shown large on the right. Click anyone to open their profile.

Mail

Edit the wording of automatic emails, and send one message to a group of users.

Automatic email wording
⚠️
These are the real emails customers receive automatically. Saving here changes what every new signup, trial warning and receipt says from that moment on. Nobody reviews it and nothing is sent for approval. Keep edits small and deliberate: attaching a coupon or changing an offer is what this is for. Rewriting an email from scratch is not.

Nothing is sent when you save. The new wording is used the next time that email would have gone out anyway.
Every field below is optional and overrides on its own. Fill in only what you want to change and tick Active: anything you leave blank keeps the built in version. So attaching a coupon and writing nothing else sends the normal email with the code added at the end, which is usually what you want. Leaving a field empty is also the safest choice, because that text stays current if the original is ever improved. If a template is broken, the email still sends using the built in version: wording stored in a database must never stop a receipt or a trial warning going out.
Checked before sending. An expired or spent code is left out rather than mailed.
Send to a group
📩
This sends immediately and cannot be recalled. It is a one-off message to real customers, not a change to an automatic email. Read the preview, check the recipient count, and remember that people who have unsubscribed or asked for their account to be deleted are already excluded.
One message to everyone in a group. This is marketing rather than transactional, so it skips anyone who has opted out and carries an unsubscribe link. That link is not a formality: promotional mail without an easy way out gets reported as spam, and the domain carrying those complaints is the same one that carries your password resets and receipts. Damaging it breaks sign in for everybody.
Placeholders: {{name}} their first name, {{coupon}} the code above.
