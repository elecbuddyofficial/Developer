Revenue

Every payment that actually took money, read straight from the Razorpay records rather than copied. Abandoned checkouts are not here: 22 of them exist, and counting them would report about six times the real income.

This differs from the console's Payments tab, on purpose
The Payments tab leaves refunds out of its revenue figure and shows them separately. A profit and loss statement nets them off instead, so the two numbers will not match. Both are right for what they are.
