Reverse Power Relay
[TABLE]
REVERSE POWER RELAY — Complete Answer
PURPOSE: Trips the generator ACB when the generator absorbs power from the busbar instead of delivering power. This means the prime mover has failed but generator remains connected — it motors (is driven) by the busbar.

WHAT CAUSES REVERSE POWER:
   Prime mover (diesel engine) fuel supply fails or trips.
   Engine governor malfunctions and closes fuel — engine slows.
   Coupling failure between engine and generator.
   Generator coasts down — busbar drives it as motor.

WHAT HAPPENS IF NO REVERSE POWER PROTECTION:
   Generator motored by busbar — mechanical damage to engine (injectors, valves, pistons run without combustion — no lubrication from combustion).
   Engine could be severely damaged.
   Overloads running generators.

RELAY SETTING: Typically 5-10% of rated generator kW in reverse direction.
   Time delay: 3-5 seconds — prevents nuisance trip during paralleling transients.

TESTING THE REVERSE POWER RELAY:
   METHOD 1 — When VCB is racked out (test position): inject simulated reverse power signal (voltage + current at required phase angle for reverse power) into relay input. Verify relay operates at set percentage.
   METHOD 2 — During shore power transfer: when ship switches from generators to shore power, brief reverse power may flow — relay should operate — note the reading.
   METHOD 3 — Power relay test set: specialised equipment applies correct voltage and current phasors at required angle to simulate reverse power.
