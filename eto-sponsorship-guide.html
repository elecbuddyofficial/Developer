The Full Picture

What the ETO cadetship is, how sponsorship works in India, and what the selection process actually tests.

What is an ETO?

An Electro-Technical Officer (ETO) is the dedicated electrical and electronics specialist on a merchant ship. Every modern vessel runs on large electrical systems: high-voltage switchboards, automation PLCs, navigation electronics (ECDIS, RADAR, AIS), refrigeration, and satellite communications. The ETO is responsible for maintaining and troubleshooting all of it. The rank was formalized under the STCW Manila Amendments 2010 and is now a globally recognized competency. A certified ETO on a foreign-going vessel earns a minimum of $4,000 to $5,000 per month, tax-free, on 4 to 5 month contracts followed by equivalent leave ashore. On tankers this rises to $8,000 to $10,000, passenger vessels go up to $18,000, and luxury yachts pay even higher.

What Sponsorship Actually Means

In the Indian DG Shipping context

Sponsorship means a shipping company agrees to take you as their ETO cadet and give you a berth (a confirmed position) on their vessel after you complete your training and examinations. The core benefit is a guaranteed job placement, which is otherwise nearly impossible to get as a fresh candidate with no sea experience.

Important: Sponsorship terms vary by company. Whether a company covers your pre-sea course fees, pays a campus stipend, or reimburses any costs is defined in your individual training agreement. Do not assume any financial support beyond the sea-phase trainee allowance without confirming it in writing. Never pay a fee to anyone for a "sponsorship guarantee" before you hold an actual sponsorship letter from the company itself.

The Cadetship Pathway

From interview to certified ETO officer
1
Sponsorship Interview
Written aptitude test, group discussion, technical interview (12th-level electrical), and HR interview. Clear all rounds and you receive a joining letter with your training agreement.
2
Pre-Sea Training, 4 Months
Residential training at a DGS-approved maritime institute. Covers electrical engineering theory, practical labs, and workshop training. Whether course fees are covered depends on the company's training agreement.
3
Sea Cadetship, 6 to 8 Months
First time at sea aboard one of the company's vessels, sailing as an ETO cadet under a qualified ETO or Chief Engineer while maintaining your Training Record Book (TRB). A trainee allowance is paid during this phase.
4
MMD COC Oral Exam
After completing sea time, you appear for the MMD MEO Class IV (ETO) oral viva before a government examiner, testing practical judgment, not just textbook recall.
✓
Certified ETO Officer
COC in hand. Submit to your company and join as a qualified ETO. Serve out the bond period, then build your career, see Career Path and Salary below.

The Selection Process

Three rounds. Each one eliminates candidates.
1
Registration and Shortlisting
Companies open applications through their website, campus drives, or placement portals. Your resume, 10+2 marks, and eligibility are checked against that company's own cutoffs, see Eligibility for how much these vary.
2
Written Aptitude Test
A computer-based test covering Quantitative Aptitude, Verbal Ability, Logical Reasoning, and Psychometric Assessment. Typically 60 to 90 minutes, though Synergy's own runs a full 3 hours, see Companies below.
3
Interview Day
Candidates who clear the written test attend a single event covering Group Discussion, Technical Interview, and HR Interview, either as separate panels or combined, depending on the company.
Eligibility

Eligibility, Company by Company

DGS sets the floor. Individual companies and institutes set their own, stricter bar on top of it, most visibly on age. Confirm the specific company's current notice before assuming any figure below still applies to you.

The DGS Floor

Education
B.E. or B.Tech in Electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation, or Electronics & Telecommunication engineering. Most institutes require 50% to 60% aggregate and will not accept a distance-learning or open-university degree.
English
Minimum 50% marks in English at either 10th or 12th standard. DGS also permits an alternate route: 60% in your Diploma or 50% in your Degree, provided the medium of instruction was English throughout.
Medical Fitness
Eyesight 6/12 in each eye without correction, maximum refractive power +2.5, and zero colour blindness on the Ishihara test. General fitness and a weight-for-height check are also assessed at your DGS-approved medical examination.

Runs its own all-India entrance test: a proctored, 3-hour, 200-question computer-based exam, 100 questions on core Electrical/Electronics knowledge and 100 on English and aptitude, with 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer. One of the larger, faster-growing third-party ship managers, which in practice means broader fleet exposure than a single-owner sponsor.
