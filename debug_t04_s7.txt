GROUP STARTER PANEL — Anil Deswal Asks This
A GROUP STARTER PANEL (or Motor Control Centre — MCC) is a compact assembly housing multiple motor starters in a single enclosure, rather than individual starters scattered throughout the ship.

CONTENTS PER STARTER UNIT:
   Incoming fuse or MCB (short circuit protection).
   Main contactor (switching).
   Overload relay (thermal or electronic).
   Control circuit (start/stop push buttons or remote control).
   Indication lamps (run/stop/fault).

COLOUR CODING ON GROUP STARTER PANEL (IEC 60073):
   GREEN lamp: Motor STOPPED (safe state — not running).
   RED lamp: Motor RUNNING.
   AMBER/YELLOW lamp: FAULT or ALARM condition.
   WHITE lamp: Power available / supply healthy.
   BLUE lamp: Mandatory action required (sometimes used for remote/auto mode indication).

PUSH BUTTONS (IEC 60073):
   GREEN push button: START.
   RED push button: STOP / EMERGENCY STOP.

ADVANTAGES OF GROUP STARTER PANEL:
   Centralised control — all starters in one location (ECR or local panel room).
   Easier maintenance — all starters accessible in one place.
   Compact — less space than individual starters.
   Common busbars — efficient power distribution.
   Remote start/stop from ECR — supports UMS operation.

EXAMINER QUESTION | IDEAL ANSWER
What is a bus tie breaker and when is it used? | Bus tie breaker (BTB) connects two separate MSB sections. Normally OPEN for maximum fault isolation (fault on one section doesn't affect other). Closed when: one generator cannot handle its section load, generator transfer between sections, emergency — generator on one section fails (close BTB to supply that section from other generator). Large ships run with sections split for maximum security. Emergency: close BTB to restore power to section that lost its generator.
What is a group starter panel and what do the colours mean? | Group starter panel (Motor Control Centre) houses multiple motor starters in one enclosure with common busbars. Each unit has fuse, contactor, overload relay, control circuit. Colour coding per IEC 60073: GREEN lamp = motor stopped (safe). RED lamp = motor running. AMBER = fault/alarm. WHITE = power available. Push buttons: GREEN = start, RED = stop/emergency stop. Advantages: centralised control, easier maintenance, compact, remote operation possible for UMS.

EMERGENCY GENERATOR — Complete SOLAS Requirements
SOLAS Chapter II-1, Part D, Regulation 42 & 43:

LOCATION:
   Emergency generator must be ABOVE the bulkhead deck (freeboard deck).
   Must be OUTSIDE the main engine room — in a separate space not sharing a common boundary with the engine room.
   Purpose: if main engine room floods or catches fire — emergency generator remains unaffected.

AUTO-START REQUIREMENT:
   CARGO SHIPS: Emergency generator must auto-start AND supply essential loads within 45 SECONDS of main power failure.
   PASSENGER SHIPS: Within 30 SECONDS.

FUEL SUPPLY:
   Sufficient fuel for 18 HOURS of continuous operation (cargo ships).
   36 HOURS for passenger ships.
   Fuel tank must NOT be the main engine room fuel tank — separate dedicated supply or separate section.

STARTING SYSTEM:
   Must have TWO independent means of starting:
   1. Battery start (dedicated batteries — NOT used for any other purpose).
   2. Hydraulic start, pneumatic start, or another battery bank.
   Starting batteries: kept charged by trickle charger from main power. Also charged by emergency generator when running.

ESSENTIAL SERVICES SUPPLIED BY EMERGENCY GENERATOR:
   Navigation lights and shapes.
   GMDSS radio equipment.
   Fire detection and alarm system.
   Emergency bilge pumping system.
   Watertight door operation and indicators.
   Emergency lighting throughout ship (accommodation, escape routes, muster stations).
   Steering gear (transitional source if emergency generator not yet running — 30 seconds from batteries).
   Fire pump (1 dedicated fire pump motor).
   Sprinkler system.
   Daylight signalling lamp.

TRANSITIONAL SOURCE OF POWER:
   Batteries that supply essential loads for minimum 30 minutes while emergency generator starts.
   Supplies: steering gear, navigation lights, GMDSS, internal comms, emergency lighting.
   Automatic switchover — no manual action required.
