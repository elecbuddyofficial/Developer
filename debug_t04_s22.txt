Preparation for MSB Survey — Class Survey Checklist
🔴 EXAM CRITICAL: Deswal, Praveen Nair — "how do you prepare for MSB survey?"
1. Clean all busbars — remove dust, oil, oxidation from copper bars
2. Check and tighten all busbar connections — loose joints cause hot spots (thermal imaging useful)
3. Test all relays: OCR, under-voltage, reverse power, preferential trip — with relay test set
4. Test circuit breakers: open/close operation, manual trip, relay-initiated trip
5. IR test (megger) each feeder circuit — record all values, ensure >1 MΩ
6. Check earth fault lamps and IRM calibration
7. Inspect all cable entries and glands — no oil ingress, correct sealing compound
8. Verify instruments: voltmeters, ammeters, kWh meters — calibration due?
9. Check synchroscope and synchronising lamps operation
10. Test all alarms and annunciators from MSB panel
11. Verify ACB racking mechanism — smooth in/out, interlocks function correctly
12. Check all labels, cable tags, and circuit identification are correct and legible
13. Ensure rubber mats are in place — correct thickness and voltage rating
14. Prepare all maintenance records and test certificates for surveyor review
